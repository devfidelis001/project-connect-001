About Your Labour Market

Connecting People With Opportunities

Your Labour Market is a Nigerian employment and professional networking platform created to make it easier for job seekers, skilled professionals, employers, recruiters, and businesses to connect with one another.

The idea behind Your Labour Market is simple: finding a job should be easier, discovering talent should be faster, and professional connections should be more accessible.

Instead of having job seekers depend entirely on scattered job advertisements, social media posts, or personal connections, Your Labour Market brings employment opportunities and professional interactions together in one platform.

Whether you are actively searching for your next job, looking for skilled workers, building your professional profile, or recruiting candidates for your organization, Your Labour Market provides a simple place to connect.

What We Are Building

Your Labour Market is more than just a job board.

The platform combines job discovery, applications, professional profiles, recruiter communication, notifications, and social interaction to create a more connected employment experience.

Users can discover available jobs, view opportunities, apply for positions, communicate with recruiters, create professional profiles, and stay informed about activities that matter to their careers.

Employers and recruiters can use the platform to reach potential candidates and make their recruitment process more direct.

Our goal is to continue developing the platform into a trusted digital environment where talent meets opportunity.

Our Mission

Our mission is to connect job seekers with genuine opportunities and help employers discover talented people more efficiently.

We believe that technology can make the employment process more accessible.

A person looking for work should be able to discover opportunities without having to search endlessly through different platforms. A skilled professional should have a place to showcase their abilities. A recruiter should be able to find potential candidates and communicate with them directly. And employers should have access to a growing community of people looking for opportunities.

Your Labour Market is being built around these ideas.

Features of Your Labour Market
  • Job Discovery — Users can explore available employment opportunities and discover jobs that match their interests, skills, and career goals.
  • Job Applications — Job seekers can apply for available positions directly through the platform, making the process more convenient and organized.
  • Direct Messaging — Recruiters and candidates can communicate directly, allowing important conversations to happen without unnecessary barriers.
  • Professional Profiles — Users can create public profiles where they can showcase their skills, professional information, experience, and other details that can help employers understand their background.
  • Notifications — Users can stay informed about important activities, including new opportunities and messages from recruiters.
  • Employer & Recruiter Connections — Recruiters and employers can connect with potential candidates and discover people with the skills they need.
  • Simple User Experience — Your Labour Market is designed with simplicity in mind, making it straightforward to find jobs, build a professional presence, and connect with other users.
Why Your Labour Market Exists

The employment process can be difficult for both job seekers and employers. People looking for work may struggle to discover suitable opportunities, while businesses and recruiters may struggle to reach the right candidates.

Your Labour Market aims to reduce this gap by creating a platform where job seekers, professionals, recruiters, and employers can meet in one place.

We are building the platform with the Nigerian employment environment in mind while creating a foundation that can eventually serve users in other locations as well.

The Vision

The vision for Your Labour Market is to grow from an employment platform into a broader professional and career ecosystem.

We want users to be able to discover jobs, build professional identities, communicate with recruiters, develop valuable connections, and discover new career opportunities from one platform.

As the platform develops, we will continue improving its features, reliability, user experience, and opportunities available to its community.

"Connect people. Discover opportunities. Build careers."

About the Founder
Fidelis Francis Fidelis

Founder

Your Labour Market was founded by Fidelis Francis Fidelis, a young Nigerian technology enthusiast and entrepreneur with an interest in programming, digital products, business, and solving practical problems through technology.

Fidelis is the person behind the development and vision of Your Labour Market. His interest in technology began with programming and gradually developed into a larger interest in entrepreneurship and building digital platforms that can solve real-world problems.

Rather than simply learning programming as a skill, Fidelis wanted to use technology to create something useful. That vision became Your Labour Market.

The platform represents his interest in combining technology, employment, entrepreneurship, and professional networking into one practical product. As the platform continues to grow, the goal is to make Your Labour Market useful to people across Nigeria and eventually expand its reach beyond its initial market.
